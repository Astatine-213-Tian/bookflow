# Book preparation

A personal library gathers books and later additions from different sources,
preserves their reading structure, and prepares editions for reading or editing.

## Language

**Book**: The work being collected or maintained, identified independently of its
display title and where a reader accesses it.

**Source**: Material used to acquire or correct a book, including a website,
an existing edition, a text file, or supplied images.
_Avoid_: Import as a name for every acquisition task.

**Search**: Discovery and preview of possible sources before choosing one.
_Avoid_: Crawl when only finding candidates.

**Collection**: Retrieval of content from a chosen source within an explicit book
or collection scope. A known Patreon collection does not require search.

**Chapter**: A titled reading unit. Introductions and afterwords have their own
roles and remain distinct from the first or last story chapter.

**Volume**: An ordered group of chapters with a shared heading. A volume called
番外 does not, by its name alone, become an independent extra.

**Independent extra**: Supplementary content outside a book's main outline,
potentially shared by several books.
_Avoid_: Treating every afterword or volume named 番外 as a shared extra.

**Normalization**: Agreed corrections to characters, spacing, punctuation and
explicit formatting that preserve the meaning and selected content.
_Avoid_: Translation, rewriting, source selection.

**Correction**: A reviewed replacement for identified content, based on its
current version and preserving unrelated reading units.

**Destination**: A selected place or format for the prepared book, such as an
EPUB, a TXT reading copy, or an editable Notion draft.
